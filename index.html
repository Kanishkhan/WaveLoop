FAQ

Questions before you register.

Who is this for?

Final-year engineering students, especially CSE / IT / ECE students preparing for placements and looking to ship a project they can explain.

Do I need advanced AI experience?

No. The campaign is designed around basic technical familiarity and a first practical build.

What happens after I register?

You receive your registration confirmation and personal referral link. You can share it with classmates and track verified referral progress in your hub on this page.

Is the 500-registration number real?

No. This is a challenge simulation. The 500 target and the campaign replay are planning assumptions, not actual student registrations.
